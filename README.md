# WIZoom+
🔍 **Zoomify Plus**

Take your **cinematic zooming** to the **next level** with **Zoomify Plus** — a feature-rich, highly customizable, and **open-source** zoom mod based on **Zoomify**.

**Zoomify Plus** is an advanced quality-of-life (QoL) utility mod built upon the foundation of Zoomify. Designed for players who want ultra-smooth camera transitions, detailed customization options, and peak modern performance without sacrificing game stability.

📖 **About The Mod**
Whether you're crafting **cinematic videos**, taking aesthetic **screenshots**, or just exploring vast worlds, **Zoomify Plus** delivers the **ultimate zoom experience**. Expanding on the core features of **Zoomify**, this mod gives you fine-grained **control** over **zoom** speeds, **animation** curves, dynamic **scroll-zooming**, and **keybind behaviors**.

⚙️ **Extensive Customization**:
Tweak FOV speed, **zoom distance**, keybind toggles, and **HUD behaviors** in a clean config menu.

⚡ **High Performance & Modern**: Optimized for **Fabric** and modern loaders to guarantee maximum **FPS**.

📜 **100% Open Source**: Built under the **MIT license** for **community** freedom and open collaboration.
