# WIZoom Plus
🔍 **WIZoom Plus**

Take your Minecraft **zoom** experience to the **next level** with **WIZoom Plus** — an enhanced, lightweight, and **open-source** zoom mod **based** on **WI Zoom**.WIZoom Plus is a lightweight, **high-performance** quality-of-life (QoL) **utility** mod designed to expand upon the classic **WI Zoom** experience. Built with performance and flexibility in mind, it brings **smoother controls**, enhanced customization, and complete open-source **freedom** to your game.

📖 **About The Mod**

**Need** a simple yet powerful zoom mod that doesn't bloat your game? **WIZoom Plus** builds directly on top of the beloved WI Zoom functionality, providing refined **controls**, seamless integration, and zero lag — whether you're exploring landscapes, spotting distant builds, or recording **cinematics**.
